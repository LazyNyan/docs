**Display your music from (almost) any music source on stream!**

This method uses Nutty's SMTC bridge program to tell our widget what you're playing on your Windows PC (yes its windows only) 

- Follow the [setup guide for his widget](https://nuttylmao.notion.site/Universal-Now-Playing-38f19969b237801e8484c9f387071409) up to step 2.
- Decide whether you want to use his widget instead of mine
  - Follow the rest of his guide if so
  - no i wont be upset its just a widget lol
- Now go to the [widget setup page](https://music.nyako.cat/setup), click "Everything else".

 :::info[Tip]
By default, Nyan's Music Overlay will automatically attach to the currently focused application.

To target a specific application, enter its' executable name into the **Target Application** field.
:::
